Couture Saree Draping

Architectural box pleating, weightless drape balancing, and steam setting for pure silk.

Sculpting the Nine Yards into Royal Silhouette

A South Indian bride's Kanjeevaram saree carries heritage, blessings, and intricate pure zari weaves. Draping heavy pure silk requires architectural understanding of posture, pallu weight distribution, and comfortable ease of movement during hours of sacred homams and stage greetings.

At Sravani Makeovers, we offer specialized box pleating, pre-pleating, steam setting, and secure invisible pin locking so your drape never shifts, sags, or pulls, remaining immaculate throughout your wedding day.
