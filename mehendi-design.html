Sacred Auspicious Henna Storytelling

Mehendi is the heart of Indian weddings. At Sravani Makeovers, we believe your henna should narrate your unique love story. From custom Radha-Krishna and groom portraits to skyline silhouettes of where you first met, every stroke is executed with laser precision.

We strictly use 100% organic, chemical-free henna prepared fresh for each client. Free from PPD, synthetic dyes, and artificial preservatives, our henna is safe for sensitive skin and delivers a rich dark stain lasting up to two weeks.
